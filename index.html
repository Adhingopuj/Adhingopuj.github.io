Yulu Bikes, shared electric mobility fleet

Ending a recurring brake plate failure across the fleet

90%

reduction in brake plate failures after the countermeasure

The problem
Brake plate damage kept reappearing across locations. Each repair fixed one vehicle, but the failure came back on others, costing parts, mechanic hours and vehicle uptime.
What I did
Ran failure trend analysis across the fleet to confirm it was a systemic pattern rather than isolated damage, established the root cause, and worked with cross-functional teams to design and introduce a countermeasure.
How we knew it worked
Monitored the failure rate after implementation rather than closing the case at the fix, until the reduction held.
