# The EV Handbook

Most writing about electric vehicles is either a buyer’s guide or a textbook. This handbook sits between the two. It is written for technicians, service engineers, fleet operators and engineering students who need to understand how an electric two-wheeler actually behaves once it is on the road, and what to do when it stops behaving.

It draws on six years of work across drivetrain design, production diagnostics, distribution centre operations and fleet-scale repair and maintenance. The focus is two-wheelers, because that is where most of India’s electric vehicles are. The final chapter covers what changes when you move to four wheels.

The chapters build on each other. The first four explain how the vehicle works, block by block. Chapters 5 and 6 turn that into diagnosis: a method for finding faults and a guide to the failures you will meet most often. Chapters 7 and 8 zoom out to running a fleet and making fixes last. Chapter 9 covers safety, and chapter 10 the step up to four-wheelers.

> **A note on figures.** Numbers in this handbook, such as voltages and test values, are typical ranges given to build understanding. Always follow the manufacturer’s service manual and specifications for the vehicle in front of you.
